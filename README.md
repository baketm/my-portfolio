# My Personal Portfolio Website

## Project Overview

For my mid-semester project, I created a personal
portfolio website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

I wanted to create something simple but also
different from a basic website. My goal was to
show my personality, interests, and projects
while practicing what I learned in class.

## Content

### 1. What is your full name?
My name is [Your Name].

### 2. What is the purpose of your website?
The purpose of my website is to introduce
myself and share my work, skills, and interests.

### 3. Who is your target audience?
My target audience is professors, classmates,
future employers, and anyone interested in
learning more about me.

### 4. What skills will you highlight?
I want to highlight communication, teamwork,
problem solving, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

### 5. What projects will you showcase?
I will showcase my portfolio website and
some assignments I have completed in college.

### 6. How will you describe yourself?
I am a college student who enjoys learning
new things, being creative, and improving
my skills.

### 7. What pages will your website include?
My website has a Home page, About page,
Projects page, and Contact page.

### 8. What is your career goal?
My goal is to continue learning and preparing
for opportunities in my future career.

### 9. What technologies have you used?
I have used HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and NetBeans.

### 10. What achievements will you highlight?
I want to highlight my college projects,
academic progress, and the skills I have learned.

### 11. What should visitors do on your website?
I want visitors to explore my projects,
learn more about me, and contact me.

### 12. Will you include a resume?
I may add my resume in the future once
I have more experience and projects.

### 13. What social links will you include?
I plan to include my GitHub profile
and possibly LinkedIn in the future.

## Design

### 14. What overall style will you use?
I chose a modern and creative style that
is simple to understand and navigate.

### 15. What colors will you use?
I chose black, lime green, and purple.
I like how these colors look together
because they make the website stand out.

### 16. What font will you use?
I chose Arial because it is simple,
clear, and easy to read.

### 17. How does the design represent you?
The design represents my creative side.
I wanted something unique but not
too complicated.

### 18. How is your homepage organized?
My homepage has a navigation menu,
welcome message, interest cards,
and buttons linking to other pages.

### 19. How are your projects displayed?
My projects are displayed in cards
with titles, descriptions, and categories.

### 20. Is your website mobile-friendly?
Yes. I used CSS media queries to make
the layout adjust to smaller screens.

### 21. How do you organize information?
I use headings, sections, and cards
to keep information easy to find.

### 22. How do you keep the design consistent?
I use the same colors, navigation,
buttons, and footer on every page.

### 23. How is your website accessible?
I use readable text, clear headings,
form labels, and keyboard-friendly buttons.

### 24. Will you include images?
I decided to focus on a simple layout
without adding too many images.

### 25. What inspired your design?
I was inspired by modern portfolio websites
that use dark backgrounds and bright colors.

## Interactivity

### 26. What interactive features are included?
My website includes navigation links,
project filters, buttons, and a contact form.

### 27. How does your contact form work?
The contact form allows visitors to enter
their information and open an email draft.

### 28. What JavaScript features did you use?
I used JavaScript for the mobile menu,
project filtering, contact form,
and automatic copyright year.

### 29. How does your website provide feedback?
Buttons change colors when hovered over,
and the contact form displays a message.

### 30. How does interactivity improve the site?
It makes the website easier to use
and helps visitors find information.

### 31. What did you learn?
I learned how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
work together to create a website.
I also became more comfortable using NetBeans.

## Website Structure

Home - Introduction and interests

About - Background, skills, and goals

Projects - Examples of my work

Contact - A way to get in touch

WIREFRAME EXPLANATION

I wanted my website to be easy to understand
without making it look boring. I placed the
navigation at the top so visitors can move
between pages without getting confused.

On the homepage, I put my introduction first
because I want visitors to know who I am
right away. I also separated my interests
into cards instead of writing one long
paragraph.

For the mobile version, I decided to stack
the sections vertically so everything
would be easier to read on a smaller screen.

Overall, I wanted the layout to be simple,
organized, and comfortable to navigate.
